# Sector ETF momentum: current framework

**Main question: does momentum-based sector selection add enough net value to justify trading costs and active risk?**

- **Setting:** a US equity manager with an existing SPY core allocation reviews a proposed 20% sector rotation sleeve.
- **Rule:** rank the nine original sector ETFs on prior twelve-month returns excluding the latest month; hold the top three equally. Decide at month-end and trade at the following session's close.
- **Comparisons:** monthly sector equal-weighting tests incremental value; SPY supplies the market reference; random three-sector portfolios diagnose concentration and identity selection; blended accounts measure allocation effects.
- **Validation:** reconcile signal timing, drifting positions and costs, then assess net returns, drawdown and active risk. Check limited environment conditions, executable filters and external industry replication.
- **Finding:** full-period momentum CAGR exceeded SPY but lagged sector equal-weighting. The active-return interval crossed zero. Random controls placed returns near the middle and maximum drawdown shallower than most paths. No reliable advance enablement rule was established, so the proposed allocation is not added.
- **Scope:** this retrospective study does not establish that passive investing always wins or that reversal works. Phase one is closed. Prospective evidence requires frozen rules and new observations; economic explanations require point-in-time information and a new protocol.
